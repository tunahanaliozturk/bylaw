# Code of conduct

bylaw is a small project and we want it to stay a good place to work on.

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks, in any project space.
- No publishing anyone's private information.
- Keep feedback specific and about the work.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or issues that break these rules, and may block people who keep breaking them.

To report a problem privately, use the **Report a vulnerability** form on the Security tab and say it is a conduct report, or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
